# US Higher Education MVP Scope

## Implemented in the demo

- US academic-term registration, add/drop, census, withdrawal, and grades-due dates
- Subject code, catalog number, credit hours, course level, grading basis, and effective term
- Course sections represented by standard Education Student Groups
- CRN, section number, campus, delivery method, capacity, waitlist capacity, and section status
- Meeting days, times, room, and primary instructor
- Standard Course Schedules linked to each prepared section
- Section-aware registration using standard Course Enrollment records
- Standard Student Group rosters with capacity enforcement
- Application type, admit type, residency classification, and enrollment-deposit status
- Student preferred name, pronouns, academic level, class standing, enrollment status, catalog year, and advisor

## Explicitly deferred

The demo does not claim production compliance or complete processing for:

- FERPA consent, directory-information elections, proxy access, and legitimate-interest auditing
- Federal Student Aid, FAFSA, ISIR, COD, NSLDS, or Satisfactory Academic Progress
- SEVIS international-student processing
- 1098-T tax reporting
- IPEDS or National Student Clearinghouse reporting
- Title IX, ADA accommodations, NCAA eligibility, or state authorization
- Official GPA, repeat-course, transfer-credit, degree-audit, or transcript policy
- Billing residency determinations or tuition calculation

Each deferred area requires institutional policy decisions, legal review, security design, and dedicated acceptance testing before production use.
